About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu Food Festival is a special event that celebrates the traditional food and cultural heritage of Terengganu. Visitors can explore various local dishes prepared by talented local food vendors.

The festival provides an opportunity for visitors to experience the unique taste of Terengganu cuisine. It also supports local businesses and introduces traditional Malaysian food to people from different backgrounds.

Free Entry! Everyone is welcome to join the festival.

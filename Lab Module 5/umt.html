About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university focuses on marine science, aquatic resources, and other areas of science and technology.

UMT offers various academic programmes through its faculties and centres. It provides opportunities for students to develop their knowledge and skills through education, research, and innovation.
